By recorded date, the newest are GUI Grounding for Computer-Use Agents: A Survey (2026-11-04), Multimodal Embodied Agents: A Unified Capability-centric Perspective from Computer-Use to Robot-Use (2026-10-21) and Seeing, Maintaining, and Learning: The Evolution of Multimodal Agent Memory (2026-10-14).
