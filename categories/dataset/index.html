Computer-use agent datasets

Computer-use datasets supply screenshots, instructions, element annotations and action trajectories for training or evaluating GUI agents. Supervision ranges from single-element grounding to complete multi-step trajectories. The catalog lists 278 reviewed datasets, each linked to its primary evidence.

Questions

Which datasets are mentioned most in the catalog?

ScreenSpot-Pro data (12 records), OS-Genesis (11 records) and AMEX dataset release (7 records) are mentioned in the reviewed sources of the most other catalog records.

Which platforms do these datasets cover?

Of 278 reviewed datasets, 122 are tagged web, 78 desktop, 74 mobile and 34 cross-platform; 36 have no platform recorded. An entry tagged with several platforms counts under each.

Which datasets record their supervision and scale?

125 of 278 datasets have a profile with supervision, modalities, formats, splits and counts with units. The most common supervision types are element grounding (6), agent-generated trajectories (5) and interaction trajectories (4). The dataset comparison page sets them side by side.

Which licenses do datasets declare?

The most common declared licenses are Apache-2.0 (72), MIT (45) and CC-BY-4.0 (35). 90 of 278 have no declared license in their record.

How is this list ordered?

By the number of catalog records whose reviewed sources mention each entry, most first, then by name. The sort menu above the list switches to A–Z.
