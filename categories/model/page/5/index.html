Computer-use agent models and checkpoints

Computer-use models are vision-language and GUI-specialized checkpoints that read screens and output target coordinates or actions. Entries cover model families and released checkpoints, many hosted on Hugging Face. The catalog lists 475 reviewed models and checkpoints, each linked to its primary evidence.
