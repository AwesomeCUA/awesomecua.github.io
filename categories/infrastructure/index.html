Computer-use agent infrastructure

Infrastructure covers the browsers, sandboxes, virtual machines, emulators and input-automation libraries that computer-use agents run inside or act through. The catalog lists 122 reviewed infrastructure, each linked to its primary evidence.

Questions

Which infrastructure resources are mentioned most in the catalog?

Playwright (13 records), PyAutoGUI (8 records) and AndroidEnv (7 records) are mentioned in the reviewed sources of the most other catalog records.

Which platforms do these infrastructure resources cover?

Of 122 reviewed infrastructure resources, 78 are tagged desktop, 65 web, 22 mobile and 11 cross-platform; 2 have no platform recorded. An entry tagged with several platforms counts under each.

Which infrastructure resources come with a research paper?

12 of 122 link the arXiv paper that introduces or documents them, for example AndroidEnv, WildAWM and ASIL Benchmark Images.

Which licenses do infrastructure resources declare?

The most common declared licenses are MIT (42), Apache-2.0 (32) and AGPL-3.0 (5). 41 of 122 have no declared license in their record.

How is this list ordered?

By the number of catalog records whose reviewed sources mention each entry, most first, then by name. The sort menu above the list switches to A–Z.
