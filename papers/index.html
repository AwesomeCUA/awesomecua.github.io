Computer-use agent papers and surveys

A reading list covering GUI grounding, desktop and mobile evaluation, browser agents, training data, planning, and safety. Annotations are based on primary publications, with original author and date metadata.

The date shown is the paper publication date, not the date we added it. These annotations do not independently reproduce reported experiments.
