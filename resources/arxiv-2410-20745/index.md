# Shopping MMLU: A Massive Multi-Task Online Shopping Benchmark for Large Language Models

Status: excluded from the active CUA catalog after scope review.

Shopping MMLU is a multi-task text benchmark of shopping knowledge, reasoning and user behavior built from Amazon data. It tests LLM knowledge, not agents browsing or acting on shopping websites.
