# Robot Learning from Human Videos: A Survey

Status: excluded from the active CUA catalog after scope review.

This survey reviews transferring human video demonstrations into robot manipulation skills and the data supporting that transfer. Its scope is embodied robotics, not learning to operate graphical software interfaces.
