# Robo2Process: Benchmarking and Learning Process-Aware Understanding in Vision-Language Robotic Manipulation

Status: excluded from the active CUA catalog after scope review.

Robo2Process builds question-answer supervision and diagnostics from robot manipulation traces. The studied visual states, motion, progress and primitives concern physical robotic execution rather than software-interface behavior.
