## 论文与综述

- [Efficient GUI Agents: A Systems Survey of Observation, Memory, Action, and Runtime Optimization](https://awesomecua.github.io/resources/arxiv-2609-02309/) — 2026-09-02
  Surveys GUI-agent efficiency beyond task success: observation, context and memory, action, and planner or runtime cost.
- [A Systematic Survey of Agentic Skills: Architecture, Lifecycle, and Security](https://awesomecua.github.io/resources/arxiv-2608-29596/) — 2026-08-30
  Surveys modular agentic skill architectures, skill lifecycles, and security risks in autonomous tool- and interface-using agents.
- [A Survey on Long-Term Memory Security in LLM Agents: Attacks, Defenses, and Governance Across the Memory Lifecycle](https://awesomecua.github.io/resources/arxiv-2604-16548/) — 2026-04-17
  Organizes security risks and defenses for persistent agent memory into six lifecycle phases and proposes five verifiable governance primitives.
- [Agent Skills for Large Language Models: Architecture, Acquisition, Security, and the Path Forward](https://awesomecua.github.io/resources/arxiv-2602-12430/) — 2026-02-12
  This survey organizes agent skills by architecture, acquisition, deployment and security, including their role in computer-use systems and GUI grounding. It also proposes a permission and lifecycle governance framework for skill deployment.
- [OS Agents: A Survey on MLLM-based Agents for General Computing Devices Use](https://awesomecua.github.io/resources/arxiv-2508-04482/) — 2025-08-06
  Surveys agents that operate computers and phones through operating-system interfaces, covering components, capabilities, foundation models, frameworks, and benchmarks.
- [A Survey on the Safety and Security Threats of Computer-Using Agents: JARVIS or Ultron?](https://awesomecua.github.io/resources/arxiv-2505-10924/) — 2025-05-16
  Systematizes safety and security threats to computer-using agents, with taxonomies of threats and defensive strategies.
- [A Survey on GUI Agents with Foundation Models Enhanced by Reinforcement Learning](https://awesomecua.github.io/resources/arxiv-2504-20464/) — 2025-04-29
  Formalizes GUI tasks as Markov decision processes and traces agent training from prompting through supervised fine-tuning to reinforcement learning.
- [LLM-Powered GUI Agents in Phone Automation: Surveying Progress and Prospects](https://awesomecua.github.io/resources/arxiv-2504-19838/) — 2025-04-28
  Reviews LLM-driven phone GUI agents with a taxonomy of agent frameworks, prompt- and training-based modeling, datasets, and benchmarks.
- [Towards Trustworthy GUI Agents: A Survey](https://awesomecua.github.io/resources/arxiv-2503-23434/) — 2025-03-30
  Frames GUI-agent trustworthiness as an execution gap and reviews failures, attacks, and defenses across perception, reasoning, and interaction.
- [A Survey of WebAgents: Towards Next-Generation AI Agents for Web Automation with Large Foundation Models](https://awesomecua.github.io/resources/arxiv-2503-23350/) — 2025-03-30
  Surveys foundation-model web agents through their architectures, training methods and trustworthiness. It organizes research on completing browser tasks from user instructions and identifies directions for further development.
- [A Survey on (M)LLM-Based GUI Agents](https://awesomecua.github.io/resources/arxiv-2504-13865/) — 2025-03-27
  Analyzes LLM-based GUI agents through four components (perception, exploration, planning, and interaction) and the methods used to evaluate them.
- [API Agents vs. GUI Agents: Divergence and Convergence](https://awesomecua.github.io/resources/arxiv-2503-11069/) — 2025-03-14
  Compares API-driven and GUI-driven agents across architecture, development, and interaction considerations, discussing when hybrid approaches are useful.
- [A Comprehensive Survey of Agents for Computer Use: Foundations, Challenges, and Future Directions](https://awesomecua.github.io/resources/arxiv-2501-16150/) — 2025-01-27
  Reviews 87 computer-use agents and 33 datasets through a taxonomy of operating domain, observation and action modalities, and agent design.
- [GUI Agents: A Survey](https://awesomecua.github.io/resources/arxiv-2412-13501/) — 2024-12-18
  Categorizes GUI-agent benchmarks, evaluation metrics, architectures, and training methods, and proposes a unified perception, reasoning, planning, and acting framework.
- [Large Language Model-Brained GUI Agents: A Survey](https://awesomecua.github.io/resources/arxiv-2411-18279/) — 2024-11-27
  Survey of LLM-brained GUI agents that carry out natural-language instructions across web, mobile, and desktop interfaces, organizing research and industry progress.
- [Generalist Virtual Agents: A Survey on Autonomous Agents Across Digital Platforms](https://awesomecua.github.io/resources/arxiv-2411-10943/) — 2024-11-17
  Surveys virtual agents for browsers, applications and operating systems, organizing prior work by task type, observation and action spaces, model strategies and evaluation methods. It examines screen-based interaction and keyboard, mouse and touch control, alongside transferability and long-sequence decision-making challenges.
- [GUI Agents with Foundation Models: A Comprehensive Survey](https://awesomecua.github.io/resources/arxiv-2411-04890/) — 2024-11-07
  Survey of (M)LLM-based GUI agents organized around their datasets and benchmarks, a unified agent framework with a taxonomy, and commercial applications.
- [Foundations and Recent Trends in Multimodal Mobile Agents: A Survey](https://awesomecua.github.io/resources/arxiv-2411-02006/) — 2024-11-04
  Surveys multimodal mobile agents, organizing prompt-based and training-based approaches alongside evaluation benchmarks, supporting technologies, and open challenges in mobile task execution.
- [A Trembling House of Cards? Mapping Adversarial Attacks against Language Agents](https://awesomecua.github.io/resources/arxiv-2402-10196/) — 2024-02-15
  Presents a conceptual perception–reasoning–action framework and 12 potential attack scenarios for language agents, including hypothetical web-navigation attacks.
