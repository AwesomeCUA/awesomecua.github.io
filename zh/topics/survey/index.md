## 论文与综述

- [GUI Grounding for Computer-Use Agents: A Survey](https://awesomecua.com/resources/url-0579bd92efb87b26cb15/) — 2026-11-04
  A survey of GUI grounding organizes methods by learning, inference, and target representation, and reviews datasets, benchmarks, metrics, and inference costs.
- [Multimodal Embodied Agents: A Unified Capability-centric Perspective from Computer-Use to Robot-Use](https://awesomecua.com/resources/arxiv-2610-30108/) — 2026-10-21
  PAPAV is a capability framework and survey comparing computer-use agents with physical agents through perception, anticipation, planning, action and verification.
- [Seeing, Maintaining, and Learning: The Evolution of Multimodal Agent Memory](https://awesomecua.com/resources/arxiv-2610-19366/) — 2026-10-14
  Surveys multimodal memory using representation and management axes, distinguishing fixed-policy updates from policy evolution and comparing GUI memory evaluation with other modalities.
- [Efficient GUI Agents: A Systems Survey of Observation, Memory, Action, and Runtime Optimization](https://awesomecua.com/resources/arxiv-2609-02309/) — 2026-09-02
  Surveys GUI-agent efficiency beyond task success: observation, context and memory, action, and planner or runtime cost.
- [A Systematic Survey of Agentic Skills: Architecture, Lifecycle, and Security](https://awesomecua.com/resources/arxiv-2608-29596/) — 2026-08-30
  Surveys modular agentic skill architectures, skill lifecycles, and security risks in autonomous tool- and interface-using agents.
- [Software Engineering for and with GUI Agent](https://awesomecua.com/resources/arxiv-2608-09278/) — 2026-08-10
  Surveys 336 GUI-agent papers through a software engineering lens, covering architectures, evaluation, and lifecycle concerns in mobile and web settings.
- [LLM Agents: A Survey](https://awesomecua.com/resources/url-f58895265a31c37d12b4/) — 2026-08-05
  A survey organizes LLM agents by planning, memory, tool use, coordination, evaluation, and safety, covering computer-use agents among more than two hundred works.
- [Securing Computer-Use Agents: A Unified Architecture-Lifecycle Framework for Deployment-Grounded Reliability](https://awesomecua.com/resources/arxiv-2605-07110/) — 2026-05-08
  Survey of computer-use agent reliability and security across browsers, desktops, and mobile apps, using architecture and lifecycle views to map failures and controls.
- [Agentic World Modeling: Foundations, Capabilities, Laws, and Beyond](https://awesomecua.com/resources/arxiv-2604-22748/) — 2026-04-24
  Survey of agentic world models that classifies predictive capabilities and governing laws, including the digital setting of web and GUI agents.
- [A Survey on Long-Term Memory Security in LLM Agents: Attacks, Defenses, and Governance Across the Memory Lifecycle](https://awesomecua.com/resources/arxiv-2604-16548/) — 2026-04-17
  Organizes security risks and defenses for persistent agent memory into six lifecycle phases and proposes five verifiable governance primitives.
- [The Evolution of Tool Use in LLM Agents: From Single-Tool Call to Multi-Tool Orchestration](https://awesomecua.com/resources/arxiv-2603-22862/) — 2026-03-24
  Surveys multi-tool LLM agents across planning, training, safety, efficiency, and evaluation, including GUI and mobile systems among its application areas.
- [Agent Skills for Large Language Models: Architecture, Acquisition, Security, and the Path Forward](https://awesomecua.com/resources/arxiv-2602-12430/) — 2026-02-12
  This survey organizes agent skills by architecture, acquisition, deployment and security, including their role in computer-use systems and GUI grounding. It also proposes a permission and lifecycle governance framework for skill deployment.
- [Agentic Artificial Intelligence (AI): Architectures, Taxonomies, and Evaluation of Large Language Model Agents](https://awesomecua.com/resources/arxiv-2601-12560/) — 2026-01-18
  Surveys LLM agent architectures, operating environments and evaluation, covering web navigation and native computer use alongside other agent domains.
- [A Survey on Benchmarks of LLM-based GUI Agents](https://awesomecua.com/resources/url-e1842006eb7b4745da03/) — 2025-12-16
  Surveys LLM-based GUI agent benchmarks in three task tiers (grounding and QA, multi-step navigation, open-world environments), comparing their datasets, environments and metrics, and outlines open evaluation gaps.
- [Beyond Pipelines: A Survey of the Paradigm Shift toward Model-Native Agentic AI](https://awesomecua.com/resources/arxiv-2510-16720/) — 2025-10-19
  Surveys model-native agentic AI and the role of RL in learned planning, tool use and memory, covering GUI agents as one application alongside deep-research agents.
- [OS Agents: A Survey on MLLM-based Agents for General Computing Devices Use](https://awesomecua.com/resources/arxiv-2508-04482/) — 2025-08-06
  Surveys agents that operate computers and phones through operating-system interfaces, covering components, capabilities, foundation models, frameworks, and benchmarks.
- [A Survey on the Safety and Security Threats of Computer-Using Agents: JARVIS or Ultron?](https://awesomecua.com/resources/arxiv-2505-10924/) — 2025-05-16
  Systematizes safety and security threats to computer-using agents, with taxonomies of threats and defensive strategies.
- [A Survey on GUI Agents with Foundation Models Enhanced by Reinforcement Learning](https://awesomecua.com/resources/arxiv-2504-20464/) — 2025-04-29
  Formalizes GUI tasks as Markov decision processes and traces agent training from prompting through supervised fine-tuning to reinforcement learning.
- [LLM-Powered GUI Agents in Phone Automation: Surveying Progress and Prospects](https://awesomecua.com/resources/arxiv-2504-19838/) — 2025-04-28
  Reviews LLM-driven phone GUI agents with a taxonomy of agent frameworks, prompt- and training-based modeling, datasets, and benchmarks.
- [Towards Trustworthy GUI Agents: A Survey](https://awesomecua.com/resources/arxiv-2503-23434/) — 2025-03-30
  Frames GUI-agent trustworthiness as an execution gap and reviews failures, attacks, and defenses across perception, reasoning, and interaction.
- [A Survey of WebAgents: Towards Next-Generation AI Agents for Web Automation with Large Foundation Models](https://awesomecua.com/resources/arxiv-2503-23350/) — 2025-03-30
  Surveys foundation-model web agents through their architectures, training methods and trustworthiness. It organizes research on completing browser tasks from user instructions and identifies directions for further development.
- [A Survey on (M)LLM-Based GUI Agents](https://awesomecua.com/resources/arxiv-2504-13865/) — 2025-03-27
  Analyzes LLM-based GUI agents through four components (perception, exploration, planning, and interaction) and the methods used to evaluate them.
- [API Agents vs. GUI Agents: Divergence and Convergence](https://awesomecua.com/resources/arxiv-2503-11069/) — 2025-03-14
  Compares API-driven and GUI-driven agents across architecture, development, and interaction considerations, discussing when hybrid approaches are useful.
- [A Comprehensive Survey of Agents for Computer Use: Foundations, Challenges, and Future Directions](https://awesomecua.com/resources/arxiv-2501-16150/) — 2025-01-27
  Reviews 87 computer-use agents and 33 datasets through a taxonomy of operating domain, observation and action modalities, and agent design.
- [GUI Agents: A Survey](https://awesomecua.com/resources/arxiv-2412-13501/) — 2024-12-18
  Categorizes GUI-agent benchmarks, evaluation metrics, architectures, and training methods, and proposes a unified perception, reasoning, planning, and acting framework.
- [Large Language Model-Brained GUI Agents: A Survey](https://awesomecua.com/resources/arxiv-2411-18279/) — 2024-11-27
  Survey of LLM-brained GUI agents that carry out natural-language instructions across web, mobile, and desktop interfaces, organizing research and industry progress.
- [Generalist Virtual Agents: A Survey on Autonomous Agents Across Digital Platforms](https://awesomecua.com/resources/arxiv-2411-10943/) — 2024-11-17
  Surveys virtual agents for browsers, applications and operating systems, organizing prior work by task type, observation and action spaces, model strategies and evaluation methods. It examines screen-based interaction and keyboard, mouse and touch control, alongside transferability and long-sequence decision-making challenges.
- [GUI Agents with Foundation Models: A Comprehensive Survey](https://awesomecua.com/resources/arxiv-2411-04890/) — 2024-11-07
  Survey of (M)LLM-based GUI agents organized around their datasets and benchmarks, a unified agent framework with a taxonomy, and commercial applications.
- [Foundations and Recent Trends in Multimodal Mobile Agents: A Survey](https://awesomecua.com/resources/arxiv-2411-02006/) — 2024-11-04
  Surveys multimodal mobile agents, organizing prompt-based and training-based approaches alongside evaluation benchmarks, supporting technologies, and open challenges in mobile task execution.
- [A Trembling House of Cards? Mapping Adversarial Attacks against Language Agents](https://awesomecua.com/resources/arxiv-2402-10196/) — 2024-02-15
  Presents a conceptual perception–reasoning–action framework and 12 potential attack scenarios for language agents, including hypothetical web-navigation attacks.
- [Vision-Based Mobile App GUI Testing: A Survey](https://awesomecua.com/resources/arxiv-2310-13518/) — 2023-10-20
  Surveys vision-based mobile app GUI testing across test generation, record-and-replay and testing frameworks, drawing on 271 papers including 92 vision-based studies.
- [Task Automation Intelligent Agents: A Review](https://awesomecua.com/resources/url-eb791c465f47690ade31/) — 2023-05-29
  A literature review examines mobile phone task automation agents, including programming by demonstration and usability heuristics for recurring user tasks.
- [Robotic Process Automation and Artificial Intelligence in Industry 4.0 – A Literature review](https://awesomecua.com/resources/url-b230a6700386b7e2744c/) — 2021
  A literature review examines how robotic process automation tools combine with AI for organizational workflows in Industry 4.0, including information extraction and process optimization.
- [30 Years of Automated GUI Testing: A Bibliometric Analysis](https://awesomecua.com/resources/url-24a139a7910431441e39/) — 2021
  A bibliometric review traces automated GUI testing research from 1990 to 2020, examining publication growth, citation patterns, collaboration, and emerging mobile testing topics.
- [GUI testing for mobile applications: objectives, approaches and challenges](https://awesomecua.com/resources/url-1c0a2ce1faf3017fa0c5/) — 2020-11
  A systematic review of mobile app GUI testing examines objectives, methods, evaluation metrics, and research challenges across 75 studies published from 2010 to 2020.
- [From Robotic Process Automation to Intelligent Process Automation: Emerging Trends](https://awesomecua.com/resources/arxiv-2007-13257/) — 2020-07-27
  A survey of robotic and intelligent process automation traces the use of machine learning and AI in business workflows and identifies open research challenges.
- [Robotic Process Automation: Contemporary themes and challenges](https://awesomecua.com/resources/url-2574c6eeee264762a990/) — 2020-02
  A structured review of robotic process automation examines software bots that mimic user actions and identifies research themes and challenges for organizational use.
- [A Systematic Literature Review of Automated Techniques for Functional GUI Testing of Mobile Applications](https://awesomecua.com/resources/arxiv-1812-11470/) — 2018-12-30
  Surveys automated functional GUI testing techniques for mobile apps, comparing the effectiveness, efficiency and practicality of approaches reported in 25 studies.
